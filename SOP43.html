Spectrum Operator Standard Operating Procedure

Handle an Office Check-Out / Phone Forwarding Call

Document when an office forwards phones, leaves for lunch/a meeting, or leaves for the day/weekend.

SOP IDSOP-43
Manual SectionOFFICES CHECK OUT
SourceRevised Spectrum Manual — July 2023

Purpose

Document when an office forwards phones, leaves for lunch/a meeting, or leaves for the day/weekend.

Procedure

1

Check Caller ID

Look at Caller ID before placing the office caller on hold.

2

Identify status

Determine whether the office is at lunch, in a meeting, forwarding, or leaving for day/weekend.

3

Get time frame

Record when the office will return or how long the condition applies.

4

Get on-call instructions

Ask who is on call and how emergency/routine calls should be handled.

5

Update Special Locate

Document the current checkout/forwarding information.

6

Notify dispatcher

Tell the dispatcher what the client advised.
