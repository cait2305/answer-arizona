Spectrum Operator Standard Operating Procedure

Announce Patch a Caller

Collect the required caller information, announce the caller to the client, then connect the parties.

SOP IDSOP-50
Manual SectionANNOUNCE PATCH
SourceRevised Spectrum Manual — July 2023

Purpose

Collect the required caller information, announce the caller to the client, then connect the parties.

For medical accounts, the manual says “Patch to Dr. O/C” means Announce Patch.

Procedure

1

Open message ticket

Collect the information requested by the account before patching.

2

Prepare caller

Tell the caller you are connecting them.

3

Start patch

Press PATCH, then TALK 1–4.

PATCHTALK 1–4
4

Dial client

Use hypertext or dial the client's number.

5

Announce caller

When the client answers, identify yourself and the caller; provide the reason if appropriate.

6

Get permission

If the client agrees, press PATCH to bring the caller online.

PATCH
7

Release yourself

Press STATUS/CLEAR.

STATUS / CLEAR
8

Document result

Review the ticket and document *Patched in the FOR field and call notes.

Suggested / Required Phrase

Hello, this is [your name] with the answering service. I have [caller's name] calling for you.

Operator Check

  • Complete step 1 correctly: Open message ticket.
  • Complete step 2 correctly: Prepare caller.
  • Complete step 3 correctly: Start patch.
  • Complete step 4 correctly: Dial client.
  • Complete step 5 correctly: Announce caller.
  • Complete step 6 correctly: Get permission.
  • Complete step 7 correctly: Release yourself.
  • Complete step 8 correctly: Document result.
