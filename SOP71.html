Procedure

1

Check Account 3400

Review account changes, complaints, and operator memos.

2

Initial/date when required

The manual says staff must initial and date that account changes/complaints were read.

3

Check office account

Review Account 4444 for applicable operator information/memos.

4

Apply changes

Use the current updates when answering affected accounts.

Operator Check

  • Complete step 1 correctly: Check Account 3400.
  • Complete step 2 correctly: Initial/date when required.
  • Complete step 3 correctly: Check office account.
  • Complete step 4 correctly: Apply changes.
