  • Always glance at ACTIVE ALERTS and SPECIAL LOCATE.
  • Use On-Call Scheduler / ELI when the account directs you there.
  • Check for IF Messages while caller is still on the line.
  • Never give out phone numbers unless account clearly says REFER or MGO.

Required phrases

“May I ask who is calling, please?”
Hold: “Can you hold, please?” → Return: “Thank you for waiting. May I help you?”
Patch: “One moment please while I connect you to ___.”
Use “please” and “thank you.” Avoid “uh huh,” “what,” and “umm.”

2. MESSAGE TICKETS: ACCURACY FIRST

  • Follow the account's requested ticket fields and order.
  • Write clearly using who, what, when, where.
  • Write in first person: “I… / My… / We…”
  • Confirm names, phone numbers, addresses and other requested identifiers.
  • Common side docs: *N/A, *VM FULL, *2ND, *3RD CL, *CANCEL MESSAGE, *PATCHED.

STAT / STEMI / “PASS ASAP”

  • STOP answering calls.
  • Finish the ticket immediately.
  • Alert dispatcher right away.
  • NEVER put these in Incomplete.

Incompletes

  • Do not allow more than 3. At 3, stop answering and finish/file them.
  • ESC twice to work through incomplete messages; finish → FILE.

3. EMERGENCY VS. ROUTINE

Emergency

  • Cannot wait until office reopens.
  • Medical examples: hospital calls, medication reactions, allowed emergency RX, surgery cancellations.
  • All ER medical calls are passed to O/C doctor or nurse per account.

Routine

  • Can wait for normal business hours.
  • Examples: billing, paperwork, insurance, appointments, referrals, directions.
  • RTN REF OFC: refer to office hours; do not take routine after-hours message.
If caller is unsure: ask whether they need the on-call physician now or can wait until the office opens. If still unsure, ask dispatcher.

4. HOLD & TRANSFER

Universal Hold

  • Ask permission first → HOLD.
  • Can be picked up by other agents.

Priority Hold

  • SHIFT + HOLD or ALT + HOLD.
  • Exclusive to your console; manual warns not to priority-hold same caller more than twice in one call.

Transfer

  • ALT + T / XFER → station.
  • Receiving agent selects with TALK; ANSWER or second TALK makes caller live.

5. PATCH / ANNOUNCE PATCH

Patch

  • Standard patch generally does not require message ticket.
  • Patch phrase → PATCH → dial/hypertext → when ringing PATCH → STATUS/CLEAR.

Announce Patch

  • Open ticket first and collect required information.
  • Call client/O/C, announce caller, connect if accepted.
  • Medical “Patch to Dr O/C” means Announce Patch.
  • Document *PATCHED; if no answer follow account N/A / LWTCAS / TVP instructions.

6. DUAL PATCH — RECOVERY PROCEDURE

1. Call caller back
DIAL
2. Confirm caller
name + connect plan
3. Place caller
on PRIORITY HOLD
4. Call O/C
and announce caller
5. Connect
PATCH ×2
  • Use Dual Patch when the operator failed to Patch or Announce Patch the caller to the On-Call.
  • Call the caller back and confirm you have the correct person.
  • Tell the caller you are going to connect them to the On-Call and ask them to stay on the line.
  • Place caller on PRIORITY HOLD.
  • Dial the On-Call, announce the caller, and get permission to connect.
  • Press PATCH twice, then STATUS/CLEAR.
  • Document *PATCHED in the FOR field / side documentation as instructed.
  • If no answer, document *N/A; if voicemail is left for O/C to call the service, use LWTCAS per account instructions.
Operator script: “Hello, this is the answering service. Is this ___? I’m going to connect you to the On-Call, please stay on the line.”

7. MEDICAL HIGH-YIELD RULES

  • Prescriptions: account-specific. Some allow none; some emergency RX only.
  • Possible exceptions: promised-but-not-called-in RX, seizure/BP/heart/diabetes meds, recent surgery, medication reaction.
  • Unless account says otherwise, do not take medication names, lab values, or test results.
  • Same-day cancellation: general rule up to 2 hours after office opens.
  • Cancellation ticket: name, phone, DOB, doctor, appointment date/time, reason.
  • Always take early-morning surgery cancellations; account instructions still control.

8. HOSPITAL / CONSULT CALLS

  • Confirm whether it is a PAGE or CONSULT.
  • Verify caller, hospital, callback number + extension, patient name/spelling, DOB, room/bed, MRN if requested.
  • Verify patient doctor against the account; use doctor directory/dispatcher if needed.
  • Consults: capture requesting doctor, urgency (routine/urgent/STAT), diagnosis/reason, and account-required fields.
  • Verify everything—incorrect data can delay care.

9. OFFICE CHECK-OUT / ON-CALL CHANGES

  • Look at Caller ID before putting client on hold.
  • Get: who is O/C, start/end date & time, and name of client giving info.
  • Update Special Locate.
  • Notify dispatcher whenever office gives on-call instructions.
  • If instructions are detailed/confusing, transfer client to dispatcher.
If you have a problem with on-call information, ask dispatcher before disconnecting the client.

10. 2ND / 3RD CALLS & CANCELLATIONS

  • Create a new message ticket for 2nd call, 3rd call, or cancellation.
  • Add *2ND, *3RD CL, *CANCEL MESSAGE.
  • On a second call, always reconfirm caller's phone number.
  • Use COPY workflow as instructed.
Manual marks this section as being updated; follow current supervisor/dispatcher direction if local procedure differs.

11. SPECIAL SITUATIONS

  • Spanish: check Account 0000 for on-duty “S”; transfer. If none, use Account 4444 Spanish Speaker locate/backup/translator steps.
  • Relay caller: speak directly to caller, slowly and clearly; use “GO AHEAD” protocol as instructed.
  • Triage nurse checking in: transfer to dispatcher immediately.
  • System offline/retry: handwrite messages, note account number, alert dispatcher immediately for emergencies, enter messages when system returns.

12. BEFORE YOU DISCONNECT

  • Did I follow the account-specific instructions?
  • Did I check Special Locate / Active Alerts / Scheduler / ELI as needed?
  • Did I classify emergency vs routine correctly?
  • Did I verify name, phone, spelling, address/DOB/room as required?
  • Did I use the correct FOR field / relay / side documentation?
  • If unclear, did I ask dispatcher before disconnecting?

13. DISPATCHER NOW

  • STAT / STEMI / nurse or doctor says ASAP.
  • Emergency call and instructions are unclear.
  • On-call info is missing, conflicting, or confusing.
  • Triage nurse checks in.
  • Priority situation identified by account/manual.
  • Caller/client request falls outside your authority or you are unsure.
Golden Rule: Account-specific instructions control. This cheat sheet supports memory; it does not replace the account screen.
