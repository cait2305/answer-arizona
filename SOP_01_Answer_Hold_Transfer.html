Spectrum Operator Standard Operating Procedure

Answering, Holding & Transferring Calls

Step-by-step Spectrum procedure for answering a call, using Universal or Priority Hold, and transferring calls between consoles.

SOP IDSOP-01
SourceRevised Spectrum Manual — July 2023
Manual AreaIncoming Calls / Hold / Transfer

Purpose

Use this procedure to answer incoming calls in Spectrum, place callers on Universal or Priority Hold correctly, and transfer a caller to another agent console.

Core rule: The caller is not live when you first select a ringing TALK line. Press ANSWER to bring the caller live.

Procedure A — Answer an Incoming Call

1

Select the ringing line

Press the corresponding TALK 1–4 key. Spectrum displays the account screen; the caller is not live yet.

TALK 1–4
2

Bring the caller live

Press ANSWER.

ANSWER
3

Use the required professional phrasing

“May I ask who is calling, please?”

The manual requires professional, polite, courteous wording and use of “please” and “thank you.”

4

Disconnect when complete

Press CLEAR after the call is complete.

CLEAR

Procedure B — Universal Hold

Universal Hold makes the call available for pickup by all agents.

1

Ask permission

“Can you hold, please.”

The manual also gives a modified account answer phrase: “Benjamin Franklin, this is ___ May I Place You on Hold?”

2

If the caller agrees

Say “Thank you,” then press HOLD. The TALK key changes to a red HOLD state.

HOLD
3

If the caller declines

Do not put the caller on hold. Take the call. If it is an account you are not trained to manage, tell the caller you will transfer them to someone who can assist.

Procedure C — Priority Hold

Priority Hold is exclusive to the agent who initiated it. The manual states a console can have up to four Priority Holds or transferred calls at once and instructs operators not to place the same caller on Priority Hold more than two times during the same call.
Current caller
→
SHIFT + HOLD
or ALT + HOLD
→
PHLD — exclusive to your console

Procedure D — Transfer to Another Console

1

Start transfer

Press ALT + T or choose the Transfer shortcut button.

ALT + T
2

Enter the station number

Type the destination console. For a single-digit station, add a leading zero: Console 2 = 02; Console 22 = 22.

Operator Check

  • I selected the correct TALK line before pressing ANSWER.
  • I asked permission before placing a caller on hold.
  • I used Universal vs. Priority Hold intentionally.
  • I used a two-digit station number when transferring.
  • I used professional “please” and “thank you” phrasing.
