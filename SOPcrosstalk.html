Cross Talk

Recognition, escalation, and phone reset response.

SOP TopicCross Talk
Training SourceModule 24
Applies ToAnswer Arizona Operators
Primary SystemsPRISM PHONES, 3CX or landline, Spectrum Agent

1. Purpose

To provide a consistent response when an operator experiences cross talk — the unusual situation in which the operator can hear another operator's call through the phone system. The procedure identifies whether the issue is isolated to one station, escalates a continuing problem appropriately, prepares the team for a PRISM PHONES reset, and prevents an incorrect reset of Spectrum Agent.

2. Key Rule

DO NOT RESET THE SPECTRUM AGENT PROGRAM.
A PRISM PHONES reset is for the phone lines. After the reset, reset only the phone connection — the 3CX phone or landline.

3. Procedure

1
Recognize cross talk.
If you can hear another operator's conversation through the phone system, treat the situation as cross talk.
2
Inform your teammates.
Let the team know that cross talk is occurring. The issue can affect the whole team, especially if a PRISM PHONES reset becomes necessary.
3
Change stations first.
Move to another station using Answer Arizona's approved station-changing method. Cross talk may be isolated to one station.
4
Listen after changing stations.
If the cross talk is gone, continue working normally. If you still hear another operator's call, continue to Step 5.
5
Contact Ilene, Eliza, or TD.
Request a PRISM PHONES reset. Do not substitute a Spectrum Agent reset.
6
Expect the system-wide reset effects.
Everyone will hear a beep. Active calls and callers waiting in the queue will be disconnected.
7
Reconnect/reset the phone connection only.
After the PRISM PHONES reset, reset the applicable 3CX phone or landline connection. Do not reset Spectrum Agent.

5. What to Expect During a PRISM PHONES Reset

EventExpected Result
BeepEveryone will hear a beep.
Active callsActive calls will be disconnected.
Callers in queueCallers waiting in the queue will also be disconnected.
After resetReset/reconnect only the 3CX phone or landline connection. Do not reset Spectrum Agent.

6. Team Communication Standard

Cross talk is a team issue, not only an individual operator issue. Inform teammates when cross talk is occurring so they understand what is happening and are prepared if a PRISM PHONES reset produces a beep and disconnects calls.

The operator should recognize the issue, communicate it to the team, follow the troubleshooting sequence, escalate it to the appropriate people, and avoid resetting the wrong system.
