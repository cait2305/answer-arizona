Spectrum Operator Standard Operating Procedure

Office Check-Out, On-Call & Special Locate Updates

Procedure for office forwarding/check-out calls, collecting on-call information, updating Special Locate, and notifying the dispatcher.

SOP IDSOP-03
SourceRevised Spectrum Manual — July 2023
Manual AreaOffice Check-Out / Special Locate / On-Call

Purpose

Use this procedure when an office calls to say its phones are being forwarded, the office is leaving for lunch/a meeting/the day/weekend, or the client provides on-call instructions that must be documented in Special Locate and communicated to the dispatcher.

Before placing an office caller on hold: the manual instructs operators to look at Caller ID because most offices can be identified by it.

Procedure A — Handle the Office Check-Out

1

Identify the office

Check Caller ID before placing the call on hold.

2

Clarify the forwarding/check-out status

Determine whether the office is stepping out temporarily, going into a meeting, forwarding phones, or leaving for the day/weekend.

3

Get the time frame

Ask when the office will return or how long the temporary instruction is in effect, if the client provides or needs to provide a time frame.

4

Ask for call-handling instructions

The office may give instructions for emergency versus routine calls and may identify who is on call.

Procedure B — Collect On-Call Information

When the office provides on-call information, the manual says always obtain:

  • Name of the person, technician, or doctor on call.
  • Start and end date/time for the on-call assignment.
  • Name of the client who provided the on-call information.
If the instructions are in-depth or confusing: alert the dispatcher. The manual says the dispatcher may have you transfer the client to them. Tell the client you will transfer them to the dispatcher who can better assist; the manual specifically says not to say “HOLD.”

Procedure C — Update Special Locate

The Special Locate contains short-term account information. The manual repeatedly stresses that operators must read it when taking calls because it can contain temporary patching, emergency, office-closure, and on-call instructions.

1

Open Locate Edit

Answer a call or display the account, then press DELETE/LOCATE EDIT.

LOCATE EDIT
2

Enter the current information

Document the office status/on-call instruction in the approved short format.

3

File the update

Press FILE. The manual notes the border will appear purple after the update.

FILE

Medical verification rule: the manual says to verify the doctor is listed with contact information on screen. If the doctor is not listed, ask a dispatcher before disconnecting with the client. It also says never give out phone numbers unless the account clearly says “REFER” or “MGO.”

Dispatcher Notification

Whenever the office gives on-call instructions, notify the dispatcher of what the client advised and document those instructions in Special Locate.

Every-Call Locate Habit

Answer call
→
Look at SPECIAL LOCATE
→
Follow temporary instructions

The manual warns that failing to follow Special Locate instructions can delay an emergency call-out.

Operator Check

  • I checked Caller ID before placing an office caller on hold.
  • I captured who is on call, the effective dates/times, and who provided the information.
  • I documented the current instruction in Special Locate.
  • I informed the dispatcher of the on-call instructions.
  • I escalated confusing or incomplete instructions before disconnecting the client.
